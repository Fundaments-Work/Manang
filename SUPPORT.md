# Getting help

* **Using Manang:** read the [user guide](docs/user/index.md) and [troubleshooting](docs/user/troubleshooting.md).
* **Something broke:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/Fundaments-Work/Manang/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). In the app, Help > Send feedback builds a report you can read before sending (when available in your build).
* **An idea:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/Fundaments-Work/Manang/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
* **A security problem:** do not open an issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
* **Beta testers:** see the [tester guide](docs/beta/tester-guide.md) and [known issues](docs/beta/known-issues.md) first.
